# What this helps you do

Explain the main result in plain, friendly English. Focus on the buyer, not on the file format.

## Who this is for

- Describe the kinds of people who will benefit.
- Say clearly who may not need this product.

## What is included

- List every file, template, lesson, bonus or link.
- Include file formats and approximate sizes when useful.
- Do not promise anything that is not included.

## What you need before you start

- Apps, accounts, devices or tools required.
- State whether each requirement is free or paid.
- Explain any limits or compatibility notes.

## Your first win

Describe one simple task the buyer can complete in about 10 minutes. Do not promise a guaranteed business, income, career or learning outcome.

## How to get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1 — Open your files">
    Explain exactly where the buyer should click or what they should open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 2 — Make it yours">
    Explain the first useful customization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Step 3 — Try the example">
    Walk through one practical example.
  </Step>

## Results you can work towards

Describe realistic examples of what a buyer can make or do after using the resource. Distinguish examples from guaranteed results.

## Common questions and fixes

<Faq question="What if I cannot open the file?">
  Check that you have an app that supports the file type. Then contact support with the product name and the error you see.
</Faq>

## License and support

Explain whether the purchase is for personal, commercial or team use. State what buyers may and may not do. Add the actual support contact in the published product page.

## Next steps

Suggest a useful next resource or practice activity. Do not use artificial urgency.
